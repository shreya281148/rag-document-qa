# Northwind Logistics - Employee Handbook (fictional sample)

This is a made-up document used to demonstrate the document Q&A system. It contains no real company data.

## Working hours

Core working hours are 10:00 to 15:00 on weekdays. Outside core hours, employees may start between 07:00 and 10:00 and finish accordingly, as long as the contractual weekly hours of 40 are met. Time is recorded in the HR portal at the end of each week.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their hours. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for absences longer than five consecutive days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness must inform their line manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of sickness. Sick pay is covered according to national law.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their six-month probation period. Remote work must be agreed with the line manager in advance, and the employee must be reachable during core hours. Company laptops must be used for all work; private computers are not allowed to store company data.

## Expenses

Business travel and other expenses are reimbursed after submission of an expense report in the finance tool. Reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 EUR. Flights must be booked in economy class for journeys shorter than six hours. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 EUR per day.

## Training budget

Each employee has an annual training budget of 1,500 EUR for courses, books and conferences. Training longer than two working days needs written approval from the line manager. If an employee leaves within 12 months after an approved course costing more than 1,000 EUR, part of the cost may have to be repaid.

## Parental leave

Employees are entitled to parental leave in line with national law. In addition, Northwind Logistics tops up parental pay to 100 percent of the regular salary for the first 8 weeks. Employees should tell HR about their plans at least 12 weeks before the expected start of leave.
